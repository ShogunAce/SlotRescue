Selected SlotRescue for a visible business outcome without needing a real payment, regulated health data, carrier approval, or an external scheduling vendor. Differentiation is constraint revision, safe confirmation, shared availability and inspectable evidence, not another generic chat widget. Measure recovered slots and conversion in the demo; measure dispatcher minutes, fill rate and no-show rate in a future pilot. Do not claim revenue uplift without data.

SES is the narrowest qualifying channel. Customer interaction moves from the email to a private web page, so inbound email/MX configuration is unnecessary. Bedrock provides bounded scheduling interpretation when available. Additional channels and AgentCore are deferred until the closed loop and submission gates work.
