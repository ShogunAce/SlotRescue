What can I try without signing in?

Start your own session, describe your availability, change your mind, and confirm an appointment in an isolated ledger. The AI is live. Emails appear as previews, and you can start a fresh schedule whenever you need one.

Where does AWS communication happen?

The protected operator flow calls Amazon SES at runtime for the offer and booking confirmation. Both have been accepted by SES in live tests. Acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery. The public workspace previews these messages so visitors cannot send unrestricted email.

What’s intentionally limited?

Bookings are recorded in a demonstration ledger; no technician is dispatched. Customers converse on the linked page, not by replying to email. Public sessions last 24 hours and share a daily AI allowance. Operator sending is limited to a verified test recipient.

What powers the agent?

OpenAI interprets scheduling constraints. AWS Lambda runs the workflow, DynamoDB stores the ledger, and an atomic update prevents a slot from being booked twice. A proposal is never a reservation; confirmation is always explicit.
